CATEGORY: overview
MISSION: apollo_11
QUESTION: What was the main goal of the Apollo 11 mission?
EXPECTED: Apollo 11's goal was to land astronauts on the Moon and return them safely to Earth. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969, while Michael Collins stayed in lunar orbit in the command module.

CATEGORY: emergency
MISSION: apollo_13
QUESTION: What emergency happened during the Apollo 13 mission?
EXPECTED: About 56 hours into the flight, an oxygen tank in the service module exploded, causing a loss of oxygen and electrical power. The Moon landing was cancelled and the crew used the lunar module Aquarius as a lifeboat to return safely to Earth.

CATEGORY: disaster analysis
MISSION: challenger
QUESTION: What caused the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster?
EXPECTED: The O-ring seals in a joint of the right solid rocket booster failed, made worse by unusually cold temperatures at launch. Hot gas escaped through the joint and the vehicle broke apart about 73 seconds after liftoff on January 28, 1986.

CATEGORY: crew
MISSION: apollo_13
QUESTION: Who were the crew members of Apollo 13?
EXPECTED: The Apollo 13 crew was Commander Jim Lovell, Command Module Pilot Jack Swigert, and Lunar Module Pilot Fred Haise.

CATEGORY: technical
MISSION: apollo_13
QUESTION: How did the Apollo 13 crew deal with rising carbon dioxide levels in the lunar module?
EXPECTED: Mission Control designed a way to fit the command module's square lithium hydroxide canisters into the lunar module's system using materials on board such as tape, plastic bags and cardboard, which brought carbon dioxide levels back down.

CATEGORY: timeline
MISSION: apollo_11
QUESTION: When did Apollo 11 land on the Moon, and when did Neil Armstrong step onto the surface?
EXPECTED: The lunar module Eagle landed on July 20, 1969, and Neil Armstrong stepped onto the lunar surface about six and a half hours after landing.
